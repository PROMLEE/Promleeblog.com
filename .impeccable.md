## Design Context

### Users
PromleeBlog.com primarily serves developers and technical readers who visit to learn from engineering posts and explore implementation details. Secondary users include recruiters and collaborators evaluating project quality and practical experience. Core jobs-to-be-done are discovering useful technical content quickly, assessing credibility, and navigating portfolio-style project material without friction.

### Brand Personality
Practical, curious, trustworthy. The interface should feel clear and grounded, reward exploration of technical depth, and communicate reliability through consistency and accessibility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Support both light and dark modes with WCAG AA accessibility as the baseline. Favor a modern technical editorial style over novelty-driven visuals: readable typography, deliberate information hierarchy, and restrained motion that clarifies state changes. Avoid decorative trends that reduce clarity or look templated.

### Design Principles
1. Clarity first: prioritize legibility, hierarchy, and straightforward interaction over visual effects.
2. Trust through consistency: use stable tokens, semantic structure, and predictable patterns across pages.
3. Practical depth: make complex technical content easy to scan first, then deep-dive.
4. Accessibility by default: keyboard support, proper semantics, and AA contrast in both themes.
5. Purposeful personality: retain curiosity and craft without drifting into generic AI aesthetics.
