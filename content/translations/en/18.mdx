## Computer security
What is computer security?
> Protection provided by an automated information system to preserve the integrity, availability, and confidentiality of information.
Protected resources: Hardware, software, firmware, information and data, and communication networks.

- The CIA triad
	- Confidentiality.
	- Integrity.
	- Availability.
	- **Additional requirements**: Authenticity and accountability.
## Types of vulnerabilities, attacks, and countermeasures
- Vulnerabilities
	- Corruption: Loss of integrity.
	- Disclosure: Loss of confidentiality.
	- Unavailability or extreme slowness: Loss of availability.
- Attacks
	- Passive vs. active attacks.
	- Insider vs. external attacks.
- Countermeasures
	- Prevention, detection, and recovery.

## Attack surface
> Every path through which an attacker may attempt an attack.

Examples include networks, software, and people.
## Computer security strategy
- Security policy: The rules and practices specifying how a system or organization provides services to protect sensitive and important system resources.
- Security implementation: Processes such as prevention, detection, response, and recovery.
- Security assurance: Confidence in how effectively security measures protect a system and its information as intended.
- Security evaluation: The process of examining a computer product or system against specified criteria.
