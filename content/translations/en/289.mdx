## Before We Begin
My university requires students to pass the Hanja Level 4 exam to graduate. Our goal is to *pass Level 4 as quickly as possible*. With so little time, focus on the material most likely to appear on the exam. On Day 1 we'll learn the *key radicals* that make Hanja easier to study and memorize the *essential characters that come up most often*.

## Radicals (部首)
A radical is a key component that often points to a character's meaning. Think of it as a building's foundation or a car's engine. Many characters combine these components, so recognizing them can help you guess what an unfamiliar character means. For Level 4, learn the common radicals first. A radical can appear on the left, right, top, or bottom, wrap around the lower left, or surround the character.

### Key Radicals for Step 1

Start with these common radicals. Picture how each combines with other components to suggest a meaning.

<div className="text-2xl">
- **人 (亻), in, person**
  Relates to people. For 休 (hyu, rest), picture a person leaning against a tree (木).
- **水 (氵), su, water**
  Relates to water. For 江 (gang, river), picture water (氵) flowing past 工.
- **木, mok, tree**
  Relates to trees. 林 (rim, grove) puts two trees (木) together.
- **口, gu, mouth**
  Relates to mouths, speech, eating, or openings. For 味 (mi, taste), combine 口 (mouth) and 未 (not yet) and imagine food you haven't tasted yet.
- **手 (扌), su, hand**
  Relates to hands and their movements. For 打 (ta, strike), imagine a hand (扌) striking a nail (丁).
- **心 (忄), sim, heart**
  Relates to the mind, thoughts, and feelings. For 情 (jeong, affection), combine 靑 (blue-green) and 忄 (heart) and picture a clear, warm feeling.
- **日, il, day**
  Relates to the sun, days, time, and brightness. 明 (myeong, bright) combines the sun (日) and moon (月).
- **火 (灬), hwa, fire**
  Relates to fire, heat, and light. In 災 (jae, disaster), imagine a stream (川) and a fire (火) causing a disaster.
- **土, to, earth**
  Relates to earth and land. 地 (ji, ground) combines 土 (earth) and 也, a particle.
- **言, eon, speech**
  Relates to speech, language, and opinions. In 語 (eo, language), combine 吾 (I) and 言 (speech) and think of the words I speak.
- **艸 (艹), cho, grass**
  Relates to grass and plants. In 花 (hwa, flower), picture grass (艹) transforming (化) into a flower.
- **山, san, mountain**
  Relates to mountains and mountainous terrain. In 岳 (ak, high mountain), picture 丘 (hill) over 山 (mountain).
</div>

## 40 Essential Characters for Step 1

Now let's look at basic characters that use those radicals or are important in their own right. Connect each character's *shape, meaning, and Korean reading*. The example words show how the characters are used and help them stick. These are common Level 4 characters, but check the official character list for your exam provider.

**[Basic numbers and directions]**

1. **一 (il, one)**
   - Meanings: one, first, only.
   - Radical: 一 itself.
   - Examples: 一石二鳥 (two birds with one stone), 唯一 (sole), 同一 (identical).
   - Memory aid: one horizontal stroke.

2. **二 (i, two)**
   - Meanings: two, second.
   - Radical: 二 itself.
   - Examples: 二重 (double), 十中八九 (nine times out of ten). The latter doesn't contain 二, but it helps you think about counting.
   - Memory aid: two horizontal strokes.

3. **三 (sam, three)**
   - Meanings: three, third, often.
   - Radical: 一 (one).
   - Examples: 三三五五 (in small groups), 三人成虎 (three people can make a rumor seem true).
   - Memory aid: three horizontal strokes.

4. **四 (sa, four)**
   - Meaning: four.
   - Radical: 囗 (enclosure).
   - Examples: 四方 (four directions), 四季 (four seasons), 四面楚歌 (surrounded by enemies).
   - Memory aid: picture a person's limbs (儿) inside a box (囗).

5. **五 (o, five)**
   - Meaning: five.
   - Radical: 二 (two).
   - Examples: 五感 (five senses), 五穀 (five grains), 五體滿足 (having all four limbs and the head intact).
   - Memory aid: picture things crossing (乂) between heaven (一) and earth (一).

6. **六 (yuk or ryuk, six)**
   - Meaning: six.
   - Radical: 八 (eight).
   - Examples: 六感 (sixth sense), 六法全書 (compendium of six legal codes).
   - Memory aid: 亠 sits over 八. Picture a roof above a pair of legs.

7. **七 (chil, seven)**
   - Meaning: seven.
   - Radical: 一 (one).
   - Examples: 七去之惡 (seven traditional grounds for divorce), 七轉八起 (fall seven times, rise eight).
   - Memory aid: picture 十 with the lower end cut off, or a knife cutting something.

8. **八 (pal, eight)**
   - Meanings: eight, divide.
   - Radical: 八 itself.
   - Examples: 八方美人 (versatile person), 八道江山 (the land of Korea's eight provinces).
   - Memory aid: picture something splitting in two.

9. **九 (gu, nine)**
   - Meanings: nine, many.
   - Radical: 乙 (second Heavenly Stem).
   - Examples: 九死一生 (a narrow escape from death), 九牛一毛 (a tiny fraction).
   - Memory aid: picture a bent elbow or a dragon flicking its tail as it rises.

10. **十 (sip, ten)**
    - Meanings: ten, all.
    - Radical: 十 itself.
    - Examples: 十字路 (crossroads), the saying that even mountains and rivers change in ten years (十年).
    - Memory aid: it points in four directions.

11. **百 (baek, hundred)**
    - Meanings: one hundred, many.
    - Radical: 白 (white).
    - Examples: 百貨店 (department store), 百年大計 (long-term plan).
    - Memory aid: put 一 above 白 and use “one white thing” as a cue for “one hundred.”

12. **千 (cheon, thousand)**
    - Meanings: one thousand, many.
    - Radical: 十 (ten).
    - Examples: 千里眼 (far-seeing eyes), 千載一遇 (once-in-a-lifetime opportunity).
    - Memory aid: think of adding 一 to 人 to suggest many people, or adding a slanted stroke above 十 as a cue for a much larger number.

13. **上 (sang, above)**
    - Meanings: above, previous, rise.
    - Radical: 一 (one).
    - Examples: 上下 (above and below), 地上 (above ground), 以上 (at least).
    - Memory aid: the mark resembling 卜 sits above the baseline.

14. **下 (ha, below)**
    - Meanings: below, next, descend.
    - Radical: 一 (one).
    - Examples: 下水道 (sewer), 地下 (underground), 以下 (at most).
    - Memory aid: the mark resembling 卜 sits below the baseline.

15. **中 (jung, middle)**
    - Meanings: middle, inside, hit.
    - Radical: 丨 (vertical stroke).
    - Examples: 中央 (center), 中間 (middle), 的中 (hitting the mark).
    - Memory aid: an arrow (丨) goes through the middle of a target (口).

16. **外 (oe, outside)**
    - Meanings: outside, foreign.
    - Radical: 夕 (evening).
    - Examples: 外部 (exterior), 外國 (foreign country), 意外 (unexpected).
    - Memory aid: picture going outside in the evening (夕) to tell fortunes (卜).

17. **內 (nae, inside)**
    - Meanings: inside, within, wife.
    - Radical: 入 (enter).
    - Examples: 內部 (interior), 國內 (domestic), 內助之功 (a spouse's contribution through support at home).
    - Memory aid: 入 goes inside 冂.

18. **前 (jeon, front)**
    - Meanings: in front, before.
    - Radical: 刂 (knife on the right).
    - Examples: 前後 (before and after), 午前 (morning), 空前絶後 (unprecedented and unmatched).
    - Memory aid: imagine a boat, suggested by a shortened 舟, moving forward through water with a blade (刂).

19. **後 (hu, behind)**
    - Meanings: behind, afterward, descendants.
    - Radical: 彳 (step).
    - Examples: 後方 (rear), 午後 (afternoon), 後悔 (regret).
    - Memory aid: picture falling behind on a road (彳) because a foot (夂) is tied (幺).

20. **左 (jwa, left)**
    - Meaning: left.
    - Radical: 工 (work or craft).
    - Examples: 左右 (left and right), 左側 (left side).
    - Memory aid: picture a left hand shaped like ナ holding a tool (工).

21. **右 (u, right)**
    - Meanings: right, help.
    - Radical: 口 (mouth).
    - Examples: 左右 (left and right), 右側 (right side), 右翼 (right wing).
    - Memory aid: imagine the right hand, like 又, bringing food to the mouth (口).

**[People and family]**

22. **人 (in, person)**
    - Meaning: person.
    - Radical: 人 itself.
    - Examples: 人間 (human), 人事 (greetings or personnel matters), 人山人海 (a huge crowd).
    - Memory aid: picture a standing person viewed from the side, as in the radical section.

23. **父 (bu, father)**
    - Meaning: father.
    - Radical: 父 itself.
    - Examples: 父母 (parents), 父子 (father and son), 祖父 (grandfather).
    - Memory aid: picture a stern father holding a switch or axe (乂).

24. **母 (mo, mother)**
    - Meaning: mother.
    - Radical: 母 itself.
    - Examples: 父母 (parents), 母子 (mother and child), 賢母良妻 (wise mother and good wife).
    - Memory aid: picture a mother nursing a baby. The two dots depict the breasts.

25. **子 (ja, child)**
    - Meanings: son, child, person, fruit.
    - Radical: 子 itself.
    - Examples: 子女 (children), 王子 (prince), 弟子 (disciple), 菓子 (sweets).
    - Memory aid: picture a small child with arms spread.

26. **女 (nyeo or yeo, woman)**
    - Meanings: woman, daughter.
    - Radical: 女 itself.
    - Examples: 男女 (men and women), 少女 (girl), 女性 (woman or female).
    - Memory aid: picture a woman sitting on her knees.

27. **男 (nam, man)**
    - Meanings: man, male, son.
    - Components: 田 (field) and 力 (strength).
    - Examples: 男女 (men and women), 長男 (eldest son), 男性 (man or male).
    - Memory aid: picture a person using strength (力) to work a field (田).

28. **兄 (hyeong, older brother)**
    - Meanings: older brother, senior.
    - Radical: 儿 (legs of a person).
    - Examples: 兄弟 (brothers), 學兄 (a fellow student addressed respectfully).
    - Memory aid: picture an older brother using his mouth (口) to advise a younger sibling (儿).

29. **弟 (je, younger brother)**
    - Meanings: younger brother, younger sibling.
    - Radical: 弓 (bow).
    - Examples: 兄弟 (brothers), 師弟 (teacher and pupil), 弟子 (disciple).
    - Memory aid: imagine bows (弓) tied in order. Think of the younger sibling as next in order.

30. **生 (saeng, life)**
    - Meanings: be born, live, life.
    - Radical: 生 itself.
    - Examples: 生命 (life), 學生 (student), 人生 (human life).
    - Memory aid: picture grass (䒑) sprouting above the ground (一).

**[Nature]**

31. **日 (il, day)**
    - Meanings: sun, day, daylight.
    - Radical: 日 itself.
    - Examples: 日月 (sun and moon), 今日 (today), 生年月日 (date of birth).
    - Memory aid: picture a round sun with a mark in the middle, as in the radical section.

32. **月 (wol, moon)**
    - Meanings: moon, month, passage of time.
    - Radical: 月 itself.
    - Examples: 日月 (sun and moon), 歲月 (the passing years), 月曜日 (Monday).
    - Memory aid: picture a crescent moon. Watch for 月 when it is a form of 肉 (yuk, flesh).

33. **山 (san, mountain)**
    - Meaning: mountain.
    - Radical: 山 itself.
    - Examples: 山川 (mountains and rivers), 登山 (mountain climbing), 火山 (volcano).
    - Memory aid: picture mountain peaks rising, as in the radical section.

34. **川 (cheon, stream)**
    - Meanings: stream, brook.
    - Radical: 川 itself.
    - Examples: 山川 (mountains and rivers), 江川 (rivers and streams), 河川 (waterways).
    - Memory aid: picture water flowing in several channels.

35. **木 (mok, tree)**
    - Meanings: tree, timber.
    - Radical: 木 itself.
    - Examples: 木材 (timber), 草木 (plants and trees), 大木 (large tree).
    - Memory aid: picture a tree with branches and roots, as in the radical section.

36. **水 (su, water)**
    - Meanings: water, river water.
    - Radical: 水 itself.
    - Examples: 水道 (water supply), 洪水 (flood), 水泳 (swimming).
    - Memory aid: picture flowing or splashing water, as in the radical section.

37. **火 (hwa, fire)**
    - Meanings: fire, light, burn.
    - Radical: 火 itself.
    - Examples: 火災 (fire), 火山 (volcano), 花火 (fireworks).
    - Memory aid: picture rising flames, as in the radical section.

38. **土 (to, soil)**
    - Meanings: soil, land, territory.
    - Radical: 土 itself.
    - Examples: 土地 (land), 國土 (national territory), 土木 (civil engineering).
    - Memory aid: picture a sprout shaped like 十 coming out of the soil, as in the radical section.

39. **金 (geum, metal, or gim, surname)**
    - Meanings: metal, money, gold, and the surname Kim.
    - Radical: 金 itself.
    - Examples: 金屬 (metal), 現金 (cash), 黃金 (gold).
    - Memory aid: picture two pieces of metal, represented by the dots, shining beneath a covering in the earth (土).

40. **石 (seok, stone)**
    - Meanings: stone, rock.
    - Radical: 石 itself.
    - Examples: 石油 (petroleum), 寶石 (gemstone), 岩石 (rock).
    - Memory aid: picture a stone (口) below a cliff (厂).
