## Before We Begin

If you're looking for a free Mac screenshot app, try **Promecapt**. You can add arrows and explanations to screenshots, pin reference images above other windows, recognize text with OCR, and record GIFs.
<br/>
**I built the app and published the source and installers so individuals and companies can use it for free.** It requires neither a paid subscription nor an account. It's an alternative to consider alongside [Snipaste](https://www.snipaste.com/), [Shottr](https://shottr.cc/), and [CleanShot X](https://cleanshot.com/). It is [MIT-licensed and open-source](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/LICENSE).

**[Download Promecapt](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/releases/latest)** · [Source code](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt)

macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon / Intel

## Annotate a Capture Before Sharing It

To show an error or explain a sequence of steps, drag to select a region or choose a detected window or element. Then add **pen marks, highlights, shapes, arrows, text, or numbers**.
You can select and edit annotations again. Even with automatic selection enabled, the drawing tool stays active so you can continue adding marks.
Choose mosaic, blur, or an opaque black box from the redaction tools. Use the scroll wheel or numeric input to adjust thickness or intensity.
See the [usage guide](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/USAGE.md) for details.

### Customize the Toolbar

**Choose which buttons appear and in what order.** In Settings → Tools, drag rows or move them up and down to apply changes immediately.
Tool buttons appear on the first row. Color, thickness, and redaction options appear on the second row only when needed.
Size inputs appear only for the region adjustment tool, and hidden tools remain available under All Tools `…`.
Turn on **Remember Last Capture Tool** to start the next capture with the last tool you selected manually. It is off by default.
[Tool order and option placement](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/USAGE.md#tool-order-and-adaptive-options--119)

Choose an **opaque black box** when sensitive information must be fully covered. Mosaic and blur provide visual obscuring effects.

## History, OCR, and Pinned Images

### Previous Captures and Their Surroundings

While capturing, use `,` and `.` to move backward and forward through history. Opening a history entry restores **both the selection and what was around it at the time**.
History also stores an image of the entire display. After handling sensitive material, check your retention settings and clear history as needed.
[History and local storage](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/USAGE.md#privacy--storage)

### Copy OCR Results

The OCR button or `O` reads the **entire current capture**. There's no need to drag a new region. To read only part of it, use `Shift+O` or Option-click the OCR button.
Edit the result as needed. `⌘C` copies the selected text, or all text if there is no selection. **Copy All also closes the window after copying.**
Korean and English recognition runs on your Mac.
[OCR updates in 1.1.10](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/releases/tag/v1.1.10)

### Work with a Design or Document in View

Pin an image or GIF above other windows. Drag a pinned image to move it, use the scroll wheel to zoom, and hold `Ctrl` while scrolling to adjust opacity.
For a still image, press `Space` to resume annotating it.
[Pinned image controls](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/USAGE.md#quick-start)

## GIF Recording

A dotted red line outlines the recording region, with light shading outside it.
After stopping, choose **Play, Copy, Pin, or Save** in the results window.
Version 1.1.10 places the stop window on the monitor that contains the recording region.
[Release notes](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/releases/tag/v1.1.10)

The number of frames may decrease when little changes on screen. Recording stops when temporary frames reach 256 MiB. Whether a copied GIF animates depends on the app you paste it into.

### Allow Promecapt to Open on macOS

*This release is not notarized by Apple.*
If macOS blocks the official download, click Done, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** to allow Promecapt. Verify the app name in the confirmation dialog before opening it.
[Apple's guide to opening downloaded apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)

<figure className="pm-figure">
<Img src="/promecapt-open-anyway-20261102.webp" width="500" alt="Open Anyway for Promecapt in Privacy & Security"/>
</figure>

Follow the in-app instructions to grant screen capture and Accessibility permissions.
Detection of elements inside apps depends on the accessibility information the target app provides.
If detection picks regions that are too specific, adjust **Automatic Region Detection Scope** under Settings → Capture.
[Korean installation and permissions guide](https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/INSTALL.ko.md)

### Frequently Asked Questions

<div className="pm-faq">
  <details>
    <summary>Is it free for companies too?</summary>
    <div>Yes. The MIT license allows free personal, company, and commercial use. See the <a href="https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/LICENSE">LICENSE</a> for redistribution and modification terms.</div>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>What is the default shortcut, and how do I use F1?</summary>
    <div>The default capture shortcut is ⌥⌘1. In Settings → Shortcuts, choose the F1, F3, or F4 preset or set your own shortcut. In brightness key mode, use Fn+F1. Press H while capturing to show or hide the shortcut guide.</div>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Do permissions carry over after an update?</summary>
    <div>Releases keep the same signing certificate and app identifier. You may need to grant permissions again when first switching from the previous signing method or if macOS permission settings are inconsistent. See the <a href="https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/INSTALL.ko.md">permissions troubleshooting guide</a>.</div>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>How do manual and automatic updates work?</summary>
    <div>Check for updates from the app or enable automatic updates. The app checks every 24 hours while running and supports automatic download, installation, and relaunch. See the <a href="https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/AUTO-UPDATE.md">update guide</a>.</div>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Are screenshots or OCR results sent to a server?</summary>
    <div>Captures and OCR are processed on your Mac. The app has no image upload service or analytics collection. History stores full display images locally so it can restore the area around a selection. See <a href="https://github.com/PROMLEE/promecapt/blob/v1.1.10/docs/USAGE.md#privacy--storage">what is stored</a>.</div>
  </details>
