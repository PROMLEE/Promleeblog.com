## What is network management?
An *autonomous system (a network)* consists of thousands of interacting hardware and software components.
Other complex systems that need monitoring and control include:
- Jet aircraft.
- Nuclear power plants.
- Others?
> *Network management* involves deploying, integrating, and coordinating hardware, software, and human elements to monitor, test, poll, configure, analyze, evaluate, and control network and element resources, meeting real-time operational performance and quality-of-service requirements at a reasonable cost.

## Network management infrastructure
<Img src="/posts/240618-113916.png" width={400}/>
**Managing entity**: The central system that collects and manages data.
**Managed device**: Each device being managed on the network.
**Agent**: Software running on a managed device that collects data and sends it to the managing entity.
**Network management protocol**: Handles communication between the managing entity and agents.
**Management information base (MIB)**: Where data about managed devices is collected.
<br/>

A *managed device* contains *managed objects*, whose data is collected in the *management information base* (MIB).
