## Before We Begin

Search for “U.S. S&P 500” and you'll see a string of similar names. The brands differ, and some names end in `(H)`. It's like ordering a latte and discovering that two items on the menu use different milk and syrup. **An ETF's name is the first line of the order. Read further to find out what you're getting.**
<br/>
In this series, we'll build a comparison of three Korean-listed ETFs that invest in the U.S. S&P 500. Today we'll identify **exactly which fund we're looking at** before choosing among them. We'll start with a table of names, codes, investment exposure, and currency hedging.

<Rbox>
Fund information comes from official materials accessed on October 20, 2026. The calculations illustrate the concepts and aren't investment recommendations. Check the latest fund terms before choosing.
</Rbox>

## Three Parts of a Fund Name

Let's break `TIGER 미국S&P500(H)` into three parts.

| Part | What it suggests | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TIGER | The manager's fund brand | The exact manager and security code |
| 미국S&P500 | U.S. S&P 500 exposure | The benchmark's full name and calculation method |
| (H) | A strategy intended to reduce currency effects | Which currency is hedged and by how much |

The brand helps you find the fund. Different brands, such as ACE and SOL, can invest in the same market, and one brand can offer very different strategies. **A shared brand does not tell you whether the investments are the same.**

<Img src="/etf-selection-01-reading-names-20261020.webp" width={750} height={500} alt="A diagram connecting the brand, investment exposure, and currency hedge in an ETF name to details in its code and prospectus"/>

The S&P 500 represents U.S. large-cap stocks. Instead of picking companies one by one, the index follows a list built according to a set of rules. The provider describes it as covering 500 leading U.S. companies. Its constituents are selected using more than a simple market-cap ranking.
[S&P DJI's official S&P 500 page](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/)

## Use the Code to Identify the Fund

When names look alike, use the six-digit security code. It's like using a phone number to distinguish two people with the same name. Here are the three funds we'll compare.

| Fund | Code | Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TIGER U.S. S&P 500 | 360750 | Mirae Asset Global Investments |
| ACE U.S. S&P 500 | 360200 | Korea Investment Management |
| SOL U.S. S&P 500 | 433330 | Shinhan Asset Management |

On the manager's official page or document, check **that both the fund name and the code match**. A brand in an old post or a similar search result can take you to the wrong prospectus. ## What to Check When You See H

Currency hedging aims to reduce the effect of exchange rate changes. The won value of a dollar asset can change even when its dollar price stays the same. Check how the fund handles that exposure. Buying in won doesn't by itself reduce the effect of currency moves. For background, see [dollar assets and exchange rates](https://www.promleeblog.com/blog/post/460-portfolio-reading-currency-exposure).
<br/>
TIGER U.S. S&P 500's official documents say it does not hedge currency exposure. TIGER U.S. S&P 500 (H), code `448290`, describes a hedging strategy.
[Unhedged fund strategy](https://www.tigeretf.com/ko/product/search/detail/index.do?ksdFund=KR7360750004) · [Hedged fund strategy](https://www.tigeretf.com/ko/product/search/detail/index.do?ksdFund=KR7448290007)
<br/>
**Hedging currency exposure does not protect against falling stock prices.** Check the prospectus for the currency, hedge ratio, and cost. The hedged fund's page also identifies a `Total Return` index. Differences in the two funds' performance can therefore come from more than exchange rates.

## Synthetic and Active Labels

Names can get longer because they describe how a fund invests as well as what it invests in.

- **Synthetic**
  This points to a structure that uses an over-the-counter swap with a counterparty to obtain an index return. A swap exchanges returns or other payments on agreed terms. Check the prospectus for counterparties, collateral, and contract terms.
- **Active**
  Look for the fund's objectives and the discretion the manager has to pursue results such as outperforming a benchmark. Read the investment objective and strategy to see which decisions are permitted.

[Korea Exchange on synthetic ETFs](https://m.krx.co.kr/contents/06/0609/060901/JHPETP060901M01.jsp) · [Investor.gov on index and active funds](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/mutual-funds-and-exchange-traded-4)
<br/>
Use the name as a pointer and read the details in the prospectus. Active funds vary in what they can hold, and index funds can use other ETFs or derivatives. Check whether the fund holds stocks directly or also uses other funds. The TIGER and SOL prospectuses describe strategies centered on index constituents and the circumstances in which they can use related assets.
[TIGER prospectus](https://investments.miraeasset.com/tigeretf/upload/etf/20261008090949000003.pdf) · [Download the SOL prospectus](https://www.soletf.com/ko/fund/etf/210930)

## Build the First Comparison from the Prospectuses

Leave the cost and return columns for later. Start with what you've confirmed about each fund.

| Item | TIGER | ACE | SOL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Security code | 360750 | 360200 | 433330 |
| Exposure | U.S. S&P 500 stocks | U.S. S&P 500 stocks | U.S. S&P 500 stocks |
| Currency management | Unhedged | Plans to remain unhedged | Plans to remain unhedged |
| Main approach | Mainly index constituents | Index tracking | Mainly index constituents, replication strategy |
| Next checks | Index dividend treatment and costs | Index dividend treatment and costs | Index dividend treatment and costs |

ACE and SOL describe their currency policies in the currency risk sections of their prospectuses. ACE's effective date is `2026-02-13`, and SOL's preparation date is `2026-05-22`.
[ACE official documents](https://papi.aceetf.co.kr/api/funds/K55101D78195/documents) · [SOL official documents](https://www.soletf.com/ko/fund/etf/210930)

<Bbox>
Pick a fund and start by recording its name, code, objective, and currency hedge. For any field you can't fill, write down what to look for in the prospectus.
</Bbox>
