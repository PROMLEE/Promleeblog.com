## Step 9: Final Review of Easily Confused Hanja and Tips for Remembering Them

Just before the exam, focus on solidifying what you already know and clearing up any confusion. This section reviews Level 4 characters that are easy to mix up because they look alike or share a reading.

<div className="text-2xl">
---
**[Characters that look alike]**

1. **日 (il, day) vs. 曰 (wal, to say)**
   - 日 means sun or day. The middle horizontal stroke touches both sides, and the character is taller.
   - 曰 means to say. The middle stroke is shorter and does not touch the sides, and the character is flatter.
   - Memory aid: The sun stands tall, and speech lies flat!

2. **土 (to, earth) vs. 士 (sa, scholar)**
   - 土 means earth or soil. The top stroke is shorter, and the bottom stroke is longer.
   - 士 means scholar or person of talent. The top stroke is longer, and the bottom stroke is shorter.
   - Memory aid: The earth is wide at the bottom, and a scholar is wide at the top. Picture a scholar in a traditional Korean hat.

3. **大 (dae, big) vs. 太 (tae, very big) vs. 犬 (gyeon, dog)**
   - 大 means big and looks like a person.
   - 太 means big or excessive. Add a dot (丶) below 大. Think of it as bigger than big.
   - 犬 means dog. Add a dot (丶) to the right of 大. Think of the big animal with a dot as a dog. It's a stretch, but it may help you remember.

4. **木 (mok, tree) vs. 本 (bon, root)**
   - 木 means tree.
   - 本 means root or foundation. Add a horizontal stroke (一) near the bottom of 木. A tree's roots are its foundation.

5. **未 (mi, not yet) vs. 末 (mal, end)**
   - 未 means not or not yet. The short upper stroke of 木 is extended. Think of the top as still needing room to grow.
   - 末 means end or tip. The long upper stroke is extended. Picture a tree that has grown all the way to its tip.

6. **入 (ip, enter) vs. 八 (pal, eight)**
   - 入 means enter. The left-falling stroke is longer. Picture it reaching inside.
   - 八 means eight. The two slanting strokes are similar in length and nearly symmetrical. Picture eight pieces split into two groups.

7. **田 (jeon, field) vs. 由 (yu, arise from) vs. 甲 (gap, armor)**
   - 田 means field. It has a cross inside a square.
   - 由 means to arise from. The middle vertical stroke sticks out above 田. Imagine a sprout emerging from a field.
   - 甲 means armor or first. The middle vertical stroke sticks out below 田. Imagine the hard root beneath the field as armor.

8. **自 (ja, self) vs. 白 (baek, white)**
   - 自 means self. It looks like 目 with a left-falling stroke (丿) above it. It comes from an image of a nose. Picture pointing to your nose to mean yourself.
   - 白 means white. It looks like 日 with a left-falling stroke (丿) above it. Think of reflected sunlight shining white.

9. **人 (in, person) vs. 入 (ip, enter) vs. 八 (pal, eight)**
   - Compare all three side by side and make sure you can distinguish their shapes.

10. **水 (su, water) vs. 氷 (bing, ice)**
    - 水 means water.
    - 氷 means ice. Add a dot (丶) beside 水. Picture a drop of water freezing solid.

**[Characters with the same or similar Korean readings but different meanings]**

1. **사 (sa)**
   - 事 means an event, affair, or task.
   - 師 means teacher or army.
   - 四 means four.
   - 思 means to think.
   - 死 means to die.
   - 私 means private or personal. Check whether it is on the Level 4 list.

2. **이 (i)**
   - 二 means two.
   - 利 is read ri and means benefit or convenience.
   - 理 is read ri and means principle or to manage.
   - 異 means different.
   - 易 means easy when read i, or to change when read yeok.
   - 耳 means ear.

3. **정 (jeong)**
   - 正 means correct or upright and appears in the word for the first lunar month.
   - 情 means feeling or affection.
   - 定 means to decide or to stabilize.
   - 丁 is the fourth Heavenly Stem and can mean an adult man or a nail. Check whether it is on the Level 4 list.
   - 靜 means quiet. Check whether it is on the Level 4 list.

4. **기 (gi)**
   - 氣 means air or energy.
   - 記 means to record. It is scheduled for the next stage.
   - 其 means that or it. Check whether it is on the Level 4 list.
   - 期 means a period of time or to promise. Check whether it is on the Level 4 list.
   - 技 means skill or technique.
