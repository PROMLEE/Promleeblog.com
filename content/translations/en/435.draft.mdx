*PortRhythm helps investors manage their assets effectively.*
**One app for Korean stocks, one for foreign stocks, and one for crypto.**
I went through three apps just to check my balances.
I could tell how much was in each account, but I still had one question.
> “Where exactly is my money invested, and how much is in each place?”
<br/>

This month's investment money had come in, too.
Should I buy more of what had gone up lately?
Or buy what had fallen?
Before deciding, I wanted to check **the allocation I had originally planned**.
That question led me to build **PortRhythm**.
<Bbox>
See all your assets at a glance. Make your next allocation on your own terms.
</Bbox>
PortRhythm is a **portfolio management web app that lets you compare your actual allocation with your investment plan**, bringing together Korean and U.S. stocks, ETFs, crypto, and cash.
[Explore PortRhythm](https://pf.promleeblog.com)
<br/>
## Asset Dashboard
“How much do I have in U.S. ETFs? What if I include cash?”
You can see the numbers you used to check in each account in one place.
Dollar-denominated assets are converted to Korean won using the applicable exchange rate before they are included in the total.
<Img src="/portrhythm-introduction-dashboard-20261008.webp" width={800} alt="PortRhythm dashboard showing total portfolio value and holdings"/>
- **See what your assets are worth**
  View the value of your stocks, crypto, and cash together in Korean won.
- **See which holdings moved**
  Switch between total and daily gains and losses.
- **See whether you hold the same stock in multiple accounts**
  Group holdings by security and check the breakdown by account.
<br/>
Prices are refreshed periodically.
You manage quantities and purchase prices yourself. For supported balance screenshots, OCR can help with data entry.

## Set Target Allocations
At first, I decided to put just 20% into one group.
Then one day I checked and found it had reached 28%.
Price changes can shift your allocation even when you haven't bought anything new.
<br/>
In PortRhythm, you create **ports**, or buckets, for your assets.
Give them names such as ‘U.S. Indexes,’ ‘Korean Stocks,’ or ‘Cash,’ and set a target allocation for each.
<Img src="/portrhythm-introduction-composition-20261008.webp" width={800} alt="Current and target allocations shown side by side"/>
Charts and numbers show **which buckets are above target and which are below**.
<Gbox>
Alongside today's price changes, check whether your assets are still allocated according to your plan.
</Gbox>
*You choose your own port names and allocations. The percentages shown are examples.*
<br/>
## Rebalancing
Once you find a bucket below its target, the next question is how much you can add.
> “How much of the gap can this month's investment close?”
Adjusting your allocation to bring it back in line with your plan is called **rebalancing**.
<br/>
PortRhythm lets you review two approaches based on your situation.
| Your situation | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| You want to sell some existing shares to fund another bucket | **Sell, then buy** |
| You want to keep existing holdings and invest new money | **Buy with new funds** |
<Img src="/portrhythm-introduction-rebalance-20261008.webp" width={800} alt="Reviewing estimated share quantities and allocations after rebalancing"/>
Based on the stocks you entered and the ones you are considering buying, you can see **how many shares to trade, how your allocation would change, and how much cash would remain**.
You can also exclude holdings you want to leave alone this time.
<Ybox>
**Use this screen to review trades before placing them.** Place orders with your brokerage or exchange and check transaction costs and execution prices.
</Ybox>
<Toggle title="How are trade quantities calculated?">
Stock quantities are calculated in whole shares.
Your budget and share prices may leave you with some cash or a gap from your target allocation.
For crypto, review the amount above or below target yourself. The app does not calculate trade quantities automatically.
</Toggle>

## Investment Records
It's good to see your assets grow. But if you've also added money, the entire increase isn't investment profit.
Keep **daily valuation history and deposit and withdrawal records** together so you can see how the size of your portfolio changes.
<br/>
After trading, update the quantities you actually hold and their purchase prices.
Your next allocation review starts with those figures.
> **Plan → Bring assets together → Check allocations → Review adjustments → Record changes**
PortRhythm is designed to make that routine easy to repeat.
<br/>
## Start with a Few Key Holdings
Trying to enter everything at once can make it harder to get started.
**Begin with a few ports and your main holdings.**
- **Sign in**
  Use your Kakao or Naver account.
- **Create your buckets**
  Name your ports and set their target allocations.
- **Add your assets**
  Enter your holdings and assign them to ports.
- **Compare allocations**
  Check your current allocation against your targets.
<Ybox>
If you enter only some of your assets, allocations reflect only those holdings. Check that all the assets you want to manage are accounted for before making an investment decision.
</Ybox>
