## Before We Begin

You see that an item sold recently for 100,000 won. The current seller is asking 102,000 won, while a buyer is offering 99,000 won. The last sale price and the price you can buy at now are different. An ETF trading screen shows similar prices side by side.
<br/>
**Check the price you can buy at now separately from the value of the ETF's holdings.** We'll build a trading checklist that records price, time, and quantity together.

<Rbox>
The prices below are illustrative calculations, not live quotes or trading signals. The discussion of fund structure is based on official materials checked on October 21, 2026.
</Rbox>

## Three Prices to Know

Start by distinguishing the terms.

| Price | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Market price | The price investors trade at on the exchange |
| NAV | Net asset value per share, calculated by subtracting liabilities from assets and dividing by shares |
| iNAV | An intraday estimate of net asset value per share based on underlying asset prices and other inputs |

NAV measures the value inside the fund, while market price comes from trading on the exchange. iNAV is an intraday estimate, with no guarantee that you can trade at that price. Check the timestamp and calculation method for each figure on your screen.
[Korea Exchange on NAV and premiums or discounts](https://m.krx.co.kr/contents/04/0401/040101/JHPETP040101M01.jsp) · [Investor.gov on NAV and market price](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24)
<br/>
If the “current price” is the last traded price, your order may fill at a different price. To buy immediately, look at the best **ask**. To sell immediately, look at the best **bid**. The ask is what a seller is offering to take. The bid is what a buyer is offering to pay.

## The Premium or Discount to NAV

Suppose NAV is 10,000 won and the market price is 10,100 won.
The calculation is `(10,100 − 10,000) ÷ 10,000 × 100 = +1%`.
Trading above NAV is called a premium, and trading below it is called a discount. The positive or negative sign follows the order of subtraction shown here.
[Investor.gov on premiums and discounts](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24)
<br/>
Seeing +1% doesn't tell you that the price will fall 1% soon. First, check **what was compared and when**. If you compare a morning trade with the previous day's closing NAV, new information may have arrived in between.

## The Bid–Ask Spread

Now look at quotes from the same instant. The best bid is 10,090 won and the best ask is 10,110 won. Their midpoint is 10,100 won. The spread between the bid and ask is 20 won.
<br/>
Divide it by the midpoint to get `20 ÷ 10,100 × 100 ≈ 0.198%`. This is a different measure from the 1% gap between market price and NAV. Keep the two measures separate and consider both when assessing trading conditions.
[Korea Exchange on market prices and spreads](https://m.krx.co.kr/contents/04/0401/040103/JHPETP040103M01.jsp)
<br/>
If the quoted quantities are sufficient and prices don't move, buying 100 shares and immediately selling them would cost 2,000 won in price difference, excluding fees and taxes. If you want to buy 100 shares but only 10 are offered at 10,110 won, check the next price level for the rest.
**The quote at one price level can differ from the average price for your whole order.**

## When the Overseas Market Is Closed

The TIGER, ACE, and SOL U.S. S&P 500 ETFs trade in Korea but hold exposure to the U.S. stock market. During Korean trading hours, the regular U.S. session may be closed. The latest trades in the holdings, futures prices, exchange rates, and valuations may reflect different times.
<br/>
A market price that differs from yesterday's NAV doesn't necessarily indicate an error. Official guidance explains that overseas asset ETFs may trade on markets with different closing times from those of their underlying holdings. That matters when interpreting NAV and market price.
[Global X on valuation timing for international holdings](https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/qyld)
<br/>
Today's iNAV doesn't mean every underlying asset is actively trading at that instant. Check the latest prices and exchange rates used in the estimate and the fund's calculation method. Check disclosures and the trading screen for market closures or trading halts.

## What Keeps Quotes Close to Asset Value?

ETF creation, redemption, and arbitrage help narrow the gap between market price and asset value. Liquidity providers also post quotes to help investors trade. These mechanisms don't guarantee a fill for the quantity or price you want.
[Korea Exchange on ETF structure](https://m.krx.co.kr/contents/06/0609/060901/JHPETP060901M01.jsp) · [Investor.gov on creation, redemption, and arbitrage](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24)
<br/>
Low reported volume or a single large trade can't tell you everything about typical liquidity. Look at current bids and asks, the quantity at each price, and conditions in the underlying market.

## Five Fields to Record Before Placing an Order

Record these items together from your broker's screen. A snapshot from one moment is more useful for understanding a trade than figures pulled from different dates.

| Item | What to record in this example |
| --- | --- |
| Observation time | Check that the quotes and prices are from the same time |
| Valuation reference | NAV of 10,000 won, with its timestamp recorded separately |
| Market price | 10,100 won, with the last trade's timestamp checked |
| Bid and ask | Bid of 10,090 won and ask of 10,110 won |
| My order | 100 shares. Check quantities at each level and the expected execution range |

Record the 1% premium and roughly 0.198% spread separately. Note that they compare different prices and use different denominators.

<Bbox>
A limit order sets a price you are willing to accept. A buy limit can fill at that price or lower, and a sell limit at that price or higher. Execution depends on whether the market can meet your conditions.
</Bbox>

PortRhythm's valuations are a starting point for assessing the size of your holdings. Check the current quotes, available quantities, and order conditions on your broker's trading screen just before placing an order. The portfolio valuation is an estimate rather than a guaranteed amount you can get by selling everything immediately.

## Conclusion

NAV measures the fund's underlying value. Market price comes from trading on the exchange. A premium or discount measures the gap between them, while the spread measures the gap between the bid and ask.
**Adding time and quantity to a price comparison helps you understand the conditions of an actual trade.**
