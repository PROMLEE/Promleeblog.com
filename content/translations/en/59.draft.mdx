## Use case realization
Explains *how a particular use case is realized* in the design model through collaborating objects.
A designer may describe the design of *one or more scenarios* from a use case.
- Each is called a use case realization.

Use cases suggest the system operations that appear in an SSD.
A *system operation becomes the initiating message* sent to a controller in a domain-layer interaction diagram.

## Operation contracts
As discussed earlier, a use case realization *can be designed directly* from the use case text or domain knowledge.
For some complex system operations, an *operation contract* may be written to provide additional analytical detail.
**Example**
| Operation | enterItem(itemID: ItemID, quantity: integer)|
|-----------|------------|
| Cross-Reference | Use case: Process Sale|
| Precondition | A sale is currently in progress|
| Postcondition | A SalesLineItem instance, sli, has been created|
